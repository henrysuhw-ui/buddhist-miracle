> 〔Format example, not a real case〕

## In one line
A dying woman told her daughter that someone stood at the foot of her bed.

## Setting
2020, Los Angeles, a woman in her seventies, terminal illness, hospital ward.

## Account
Her daughter wrote that on the fourth night her mother turned to the foot of the bed and spoke to someone the daughter could not see. The daughter describes her mother as calm afterwards. "She said someone was standing at the foot of the bed."

## Their own understanding
The daughter believes her mother saw Amitabha Buddha come to receive her. Her mother, according to the daughter, said only that the person was waiting.
